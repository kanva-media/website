Questions

Before we start.

Do you just produce videos?

No. Video is one output. We start with strategy and expertise extraction, then decide which formats are useful.

Do we need to know what content we want?

No. That's part of the work. We help identify what your business should be saying based on your expertise, audience and objectives.

Do we need to be good on camera?

Not necessarily. Our job is to structure the conversation and format the expertise so the subject-matter expert doesn't have to become a professional presenter.

Can you work with our existing marketing team?

Yes. We can plug into an existing team where strategy, extraction or content operations are the missing pieces.

Are you trying to become our permanent content team?

Not necessarily. We can produce with you, alongside you, or help build the internal system so your team becomes more capable over time.
